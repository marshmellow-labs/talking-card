Help

The reader decodes the black-and-white stripes on the bottom edge of each card, like the original talking-card reader. Hold the card flat inside the box, stripes at the bottom, in good light. If light reflects off the shiny card, tilt it slightly.

The camera needs https (or localhost). Nothing is uploaded: pictures never leave this device. Add the app to your home screen to use it offline.
